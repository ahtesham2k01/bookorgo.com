Editorial policy

Sources and limits

We link to official venue or destination sources used for factual context. Dynamic rules must be confirmed with the selected provider. Guide update dates refer to editorial revisions, not a real-time verification of every offer.

Original advice

We write our planning guidance in our own words. We do not invent personal visits, customer testimonials or ratings. Recommendations explain choices rather than promising one best product for every traveller.

Corrections and commercial links

Corrections should identify the page and an official source. Affiliate links, when introduced, will be labelled. A commission is not evidence that an experience fits a particular traveller.

Images

Images are used for inspiration and may depict a broader destination or activity rather than the exact itinerary. The hero panorama is an illustrative generated composition. Photographs are hosted on BookOrGo. Original activity illustrations are labelled. See our image credits; images are not presented as customer evidence.
