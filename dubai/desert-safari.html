Dubai desert safari

Plan your desert outing, pickup and camp experience.

BookOrGo editorial guide · Updated 7 October 2026

Compare the actual package

Visit Dubai describes safari options with different combinations of transport, dune driving, camp activities and meals. Treat each listing as a separate package. A headline price does not tell you whether hotel pickup, a meal or the activity you want is included.

Shared, private or a quieter outing?

For a sociable trip, compare shared experiences and pickup arrangements. For more control over timing, compare private options and confirm whether the amount is per vehicle or per person. If you want a quieter visit, look for an option explicitly without dune driving rather than assuming it can be skipped after departure.

Questions worth asking

Check pickup coverage at your accommodation, included activities and paid extras. Ask whether transport is shared, whether a meal is included and what restrictions apply to children or guests with health or mobility concerns. Confirm what happens if weather or operating conditions change the plan.

Leave a generous schedule buffer

A shared pickup window is not the same as a fixed appointment. Avoid an airport transfer or another timed booking immediately after the advertised return. Ask the operator about suitability before paying if anyone in your group is uncertain about the physical activities.

Essential booking details

  • Read the exact admission or activity included in your ticket.
  • Confirm your date, entry time, duration and group eligibility.
  • Check the meeting point or pickup coverage; transport is not assumed.
  • Check cancellation and amendment rules on the provider’s page.
  • Confirm the final price and any optional extras before payment.
