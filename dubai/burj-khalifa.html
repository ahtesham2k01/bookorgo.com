Burj Khalifa: choose your observation deck

Practical choices and questions to ask before you book.

BookOrGo editorial guide · Updated 5 October 2026

Understand the admission options

The official Burj Khalifa website distinguishes At the Top on Levels 124 and 125 from the SKY experience on Level 148. SKY also describes access to Levels 124 and 125. A ticket mentioning Burj Khalifa does not automatically include every level. Read the exact product name and included admission before comparing its price.

Choose around your priorities

Decide whether you want a skyline visit or a more premium experience. Compare the standard and SKY descriptions against that priority. If your schedule is tight, choose an admission time that leaves a buffer before your next activity. A higher price does not necessarily make the option a better fit for your plans.

Before booking

Confirm the included levels, admission time, child age bands, identification requirements and voucher instructions. Compare the final cost for your whole party. Check the cancellation deadline and amendment rules, and do not assume transport is included.

Plan the rest of the day

Keep nearby plans flexible. Travel, queues and time spent enjoying the view can vary. A meal or walk is easier to move than another prepaid time slot. If photography matters, consider the time of day and visibility rather than treating a sunset label as a guarantee of the view.

Check current details before booking.

Prices, availability, admission and cancellation terms are set by the venue or provider. BookOrGo does not take payments or issue tickets.
