Dubai with children: choose activities

Practical choices and questions to ask before you book.

BookOrGo editorial guide · Updated 7 October 2026

One main outing per day

Visit Dubai’s family itineraries span indoor activities, outdoor experiences and waterparks. Use the variety as inspiration, then choose around your family. A shorter day can work better than several prepaid activities with rushed journeys between them.

Suitability comes before a deal

List ages, heights, accessibility requirements and comfort levels in your group. Compare these against current venue or operator rules. A discounted package is poor value if someone cannot participate in its main activity.

Compare the full family cost

Price the exact party size and child age bands. Account for transport, meals and practical extras. For bundles, compare only the attractions you would actually visit during the validity period.

Keep a flexible part of the schedule

Leave space for rest and check cancellation terms in case plans change. For a waterpark, confirm ride eligibility. For a desert outing, ask about pickup and activity suitability. Use each provider’s confirmation for final visit instructions.

Check current details before booking.

Prices, availability, admission and cancellation terms are set by the venue or provider. BookOrGo does not take payments or issue tickets.
