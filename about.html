About BookOrGo

Independent Dubai planning

BookOrGo helps visitors understand attraction, safari and cruise options before booking. Our launch content focuses on Dubai. We publish editorial guidance, not flights, hotel reservations or operated tours.

What we do

We explain choices, practical questions and itinerary tradeoffs. Official venue and destination sources provide factual context. We do not claim that every guide is based on a personal visit, and we do not publish invented customer ratings or live prices.

Our business model

We intend to introduce labelled affiliate links after programme acceptance. We do not currently claim approval by Viator or another named platform. Payment, ticket delivery and booking support stay with the provider chosen by the traveller.
