Privacy & cookies

Current website

This notice describes the static BookOrGo guide. There is no account registration, payment processing, analytics script or BookOrGo tracking cookie in this version.

Guide search and filters

Guide search, category filters and sorting run in your browser. Category links may pass the selected category in the page URL. Language and currency preferences are stored in your browser using local storage. Only English (International) is currently available. The currency preference does not establish a live price or currency conversion; no live prices are displayed. You can remove saved preferences through your browser’s site-data controls.

Third-party resources

Fonts are requested from Google Fonts. These requests share normal technical information, including IP address and browser request headers, with Google. Website photos are served from BookOrGo’s hosting. External links lead to websites with separate privacy notices.

Contact and hosting

For privacy questions, email support@bookorgo.com. If you email us, your address and the content of your inquiry are shared with our email service so the inquiry can be handled. Do not send payment-card, passport or other sensitive details. No contact form is active. The website is hosted with Hostinger and uses Cloudflare for domain delivery and security. These providers can process technical request data. Hosting services can process technical request data such as IP addresses, timestamps, requested URLs and browser information to deliver and secure the website. We do not state a fixed log-retention period until the hosting configuration is confirmed. See Hostinger’s privacy policy. This notice must be updated before adding analytics, affiliate tracking, accounts or AI services.

Who to contact

Ahtesham operates BookOrGo. Email support@bookorgo.com for privacy questions, access or deletion requests relating to correspondence you have sent us. Requests are assessed under applicable requirements.
