Copyright & takedown requests

BookOrGo respects intellectual-property rights. If you believe material on this website infringes your rights, send a written notice to support@bookorgo.com, attention Ahtesham.

What to include

  • Your name, contact address and email; identify any rights holder you represent.
  • A description of the copyrighted work or other right involved.
  • The exact BookOrGo page URL and identification of the disputed image or text.
  • An explanation of why the use is unauthorized and supporting evidence where available.
  • A statement that you believe in good faith that the use is not authorized by the rights holder, its agent or the law.
  • A statement that your notice is accurate and that you are authorized to act for the rights holder, under penalty of perjury where required by applicable law.
  • Your physical or electronic signature.

How a notice is handled

We review sufficiently detailed notices, may request further information, and may remove or restrict disputed material while reviewing the claim. Relevant records may be retained to handle the dispute. This contact channel is for website content complaints, not cancellation or refund requests.

Repeat infringement

Where applicable, repeated infringement may result in removal of content or restrictions on contributing material. This website currently has no public upload or user-account system.

Hosting-specific requirements

This notice does not claim registration of a United States DMCA designated agent. Before hosting in the United States, any required designated-agent registration and contact information must be completed. Hosting location and applicable procedures must be confirmed with the operator and hosting provider.
